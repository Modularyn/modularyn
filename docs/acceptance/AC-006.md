## AC-006-B01 — Handle duplicate, late and reordered events explicitly

- Family: `AC-006`
- Type: `boundary`
- Profiles: `reference`
- Requirements: `ARC-009`, `ARC-010`, `FND-020`, `FND-021`
- Layers: `real-provider-integration`, `api-contract`
- Security dependencies: `AC-004`, `AC-012`

### Profile scope

- `reference`: G-02 durable events on actual PostgreSQL, including outbox/inbox crashes, duplicate retention, ordering, retries and poison disposition.

### Preconditions

- The consumer declares a 24-hour inbox retention window and monotonic projection versions; event-4 has version 4 and event-5 version 5.

### Steps

1. Deliver event-5 before event-4, then duplicate event-5 immediately and at 23:59:59 after its receipt.
2. Deliver it again at 24:00:01 after retention expiry and inspect the declared reconciliation behavior.

### Expected outcome

- The late version cannot regress the projection and retained duplicate receipts produce no additional effect.
- After receipt expiry, reconciliation uses the stored projection version 5: replay leaves its value and effect count unchanged. The report distinguishes that version guard from the expired inbox guarantee.

### Evidence to retain

- Envelope order/times, retained and expired receipt observations, projection versions and reconciliation result.

## AC-006-B02 — Deduplicate contact intent and ignore old publication generations

- Family: `AC-006`
- Type: `boundary`
- Profiles: `website`
- Requirements: `ARC-009`, `FND-021`, `FND-024`
- Layers: `real-provider-integration`, `api-contract`
- Security dependencies: `AC-003`, `AC-004`, `AC-012`

### Profile scope

- `website`: W publication and contact outbox/inbox delivery, with duplicate and reordered effects on one site; no search propagation or arbitrary exactly-once email claim.

### Preconditions

- The active site progresses from manifest-1 to manifest-2; both publication events and a contact-1 event are within the declared inbox retention period.

### Steps

1. Deliver manifest-2's event before manifest-1's, then deliver each twice.
2. Deliver contact-1's event three times.

### Expected outcome

- The delivery cache converges to manifest-2 without reverting to manifest-1.
- contact-1 produces one durable email intent; downstream delivery status remains separate.

### Evidence to retain

- Ordered event transcript, cache generation observations and contact intent/receipt counts.

## AC-006-R01 — Reconcile every durable-delivery crash window

- Family: `AC-006`
- Type: `recovery`
- Profiles: `reference`
- Requirements: `ARC-009`, `FND-021`
- Layers: `real-provider-integration`, `api-contract`, `operational-recovery`
- Security dependencies: `AC-004`, `AC-012`

### Profile scope

- `reference`: G-02 durable events on actual PostgreSQL, including outbox/inbox crashes, duplicate retention, ordering, retries and poison disposition.

### Preconditions

- Use a fresh item, event and consumer projection for each run; PostgreSQL owns both business/outbox and consumer/inbox transactions.

### Steps

1. Crash before producer commit; after producer commit before dispatch; after transport receipt before dispatch acknowledgement; during consumer work before commit; and after consumer commit before acknowledgement.
2. Restart dispatch and consumption after each fault and drain the queue.

### Expected outcome

- The precommit run has no committed intent or effect.
- Every committed intent eventually has a completed disposition; repeated delivery after either acknowledgement loss cannot duplicate the committed projection effect.

### Evidence to retain

- Five fault-point traces, business/outbox/inbox row sets, delivery attempts and final effect counts.

## AC-006-R02 — Recover website dispatch and consumer acknowledgement loss

- Family: `AC-006`
- Type: `recovery`
- Profiles: `website`
- Requirements: `ARC-009`, `FND-021`, `FND-024`
- Layers: `real-provider-integration`, `api-contract`, `operational-recovery`
- Security dependencies: `AC-003`, `AC-004`, `AC-012`

### Profile scope

- `website`: W publication and contact outbox/inbox delivery, with duplicate and reordered effects on one site; no search propagation or arbitrary exactly-once email claim.

### Preconditions

- Separate publication and contact fixtures have known committed state; the delivery adapter's reconciliation policy is recorded.

### Steps

1. For each fixture interrupt before producer commit, after commit before dispatch, after transport receipt before acknowledgement, before consumer commit and after consumer commit before acknowledgement.
2. Restart and reconcile the original event identities before retrying.

### Expected outcome

- Precommit runs leave no intent; committed runs retain all intents until resolved.
- Cache converges to the active manifest and contact intent is not duplicated; ambiguous external email delivery stays pending reconciliation.

### Evidence to retain

- Ten fault-point results, outbox/inbox identities, final cache/contact state and any transport reconciliation record.
