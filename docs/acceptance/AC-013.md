## AC-013-B01 — Keep duplicate and ambiguous transport outcomes distinct

- Family: `AC-013`
- Type: `boundary`
- Profiles: `website`
- Requirements: `FND-021`, `FND-032`
- Layers: `real-provider-integration`, `api-contract`
- Security dependencies: `AC-003`, `AC-004`, `AC-012`, `AC-031`

### Profile scope

- `website`: W contact email only through the release-selected transport: durable intent, acceptance/queue/delivery/failure states, duplicates and outage recovery. Destination authority is supplied by AC-003.

### Preconditions

- contact-3 uses one recorded delivery identity; the transport can acknowledge acceptance without confirming delivery.

### Steps

1. Deliver the same contact event three times.
2. Return transport acceptance without a delivery confirmation, then repeat its acceptance notification.

### Expected outcome

- One message intent and one reconciled dispatch identity remain.
- Repeated acceptance notifications do not add messages or promote the delivery state to confirmed.

### Evidence to retain

- Duplicate-event/notification transcript, intent/dispatch counts and final delivery state.

## AC-013-P01 — Track contact intent through provider confirmation

- Family: `AC-013`
- Type: `positive`
- Profiles: `website`
- Requirements: `FND-032`
- Layers: `real-provider-integration`, `api-contract`
- Security dependencies: `AC-003`, `AC-004`, `AC-012`, `AC-031`

### Profile scope

- `website`: W contact email only through the release-selected transport: durable intent, acceptance/queue/delivery/failure states, duplicates and outage recovery. Destination authority is supplied by AC-003.

### Preconditions

- contact-1 has a prequalified configured destination and an eligible durable email intent. The bounded transport fixture declares whether confirmed delivery is observable.

### Steps

1. Accept contact-1, queue and dispatch its message.
2. Observe transport acceptance and, only where supported, the later delivery confirmation.

### Expected outcome

- Durable request acceptance, queued dispatch and transport acceptance have distinct recorded states.
- Delivered appears only with the transport's positive confirmation; otherwise delivery remains unconfirmed.

### Evidence to retain

- Request/intent identity chain, timestamps, transport acknowledgement and any confirmation record.

## AC-013-R01 — Reconcile contact delivery after an outage

- Family: `AC-013`
- Type: `recovery`
- Profiles: `website`
- Requirements: `FND-022`, `FND-032`
- Layers: `real-provider-integration`, `operational-recovery`
- Security dependencies: `AC-003`, `AC-004`, `AC-012`, `AC-031`

### Profile scope

- `website`: W contact email only through the release-selected transport: durable intent, acceptance/queue/delivery/failure states, duplicates and outage recovery. Destination authority is supplied by AC-003.

### Preconditions

- contact-4 is queued; use separate fixtures for transport unavailable before send and connection lost after provider acceptance before local acknowledgement.

### Steps

1. Trigger each fault and restart dispatch.
2. Resolve the original transport identity using its advertised reconciliation capability before any resend; resume when the outcome is known.

### Expected outcome

- The unsent fixture eventually dispatches under the original intent.
- The ambiguous fixture stays visibly unresolved until reconciled; no blind retry creates an additional message, and resolved acceptance is retained.

### Evidence to retain

- Outage/timeout traces, retry schedule, reconciliation response and final message counts.
