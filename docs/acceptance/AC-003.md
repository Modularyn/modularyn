## AC-003-B01 — Measure permission revocation at the declared freshness bound

- Family: `AC-003`
- Type: `boundary`
- Profiles: `reference`
- Requirements: `FND-017`, `FND-016`, `ARC-016`
- Layers: `isolation`, `real-provider-integration`
- Security dependencies: `AC-002`, `AC-004`

### Profile scope

- `reference`: G-02 server-authoritative permissions, resource grants, settings, features, delegation and claim and session freshness through direct API, advertised client, integration and background entry points on shared-database tenancy. Canonical Admin UI qualification is G-03 work; sign-in fixtures come from AC-002.

### Preconditions

- In tenant-A, user-1 holds item.read and item.export with warm authorization cache entries. The release's declared freshness bound B for cached authorization and its classification of item.export as security-sensitive are recorded before running.

### Steps

1. Revoke item.read and record the instant the change is authoritative.
2. Read item-1 through the direct API and the advertised .NET and TypeScript clients at B minus one second and B plus one second after that instant.
3. Start an export, revoke item.export while it runs, and request its result; repeat with user-1's session revoked instead.

### Expected outcome

- Reads may succeed before B and are refused at B plus one second on every entry point; no cached decision outlives B.
- The security-sensitive export rechecks the current permission and session version, stops at the first check after either revocation, and leaves no downloadable partial result.

### Evidence to retain

- Authoritative change instants, per-request decision times, cache-entry generations and export dispositions, as evidence for TM-G02-017 and TM-G02-033.
