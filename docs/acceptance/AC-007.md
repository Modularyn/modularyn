## AC-007-B01 — Resolve daylight-saving and misfire edges

- Family: `AC-007`
- Type: `boundary`
- Profiles: `reference`
- Requirements: `FND-022`, `FND-025`
- Layers: `real-provider-integration`, `operational-recovery`
- Security dependencies: `AC-003`, `AC-004`, `AC-012`

### Profile scope

- `reference`: Reference single-host scheduler qualification alongside the G-02 events/jobs contract: time, retries, cancellation, ownership and restart; no distributed topology claim.

### Preconditions

- A test schedule uses America/Chicago; the advertised fixture policy skips nonexistent times, uses the first ambiguous occurrence and runs one catch-up after a misfire.

### Steps

1. Resolve 2026-03-08 02:30 and 2026-11-01 01:30 local time.
2. Stop the worker across three due occurrences, then restart after the third.

### Expected outcome

- The nonexistent occurrence is visibly skipped; the ambiguous occurrence resolves once to 2026-11-01T06:30:00Z.
- Misfire history lists the missed occurrences and one catch-up execution, matching the recorded policy.

### Evidence to retain

- Local/UTC resolution table, supported policy configuration and misfire/attempt history.

## AC-007-R01 — Recover a job after lease loss and restart

- Family: `AC-007`
- Type: `recovery`
- Profiles: `reference`
- Requirements: `FND-022`, `FND-023`
- Layers: `real-provider-integration`, `operational-recovery`
- Security dependencies: `AC-003`, `AC-004`, `AC-012`

### Profile scope

- `reference`: Reference single-host scheduler qualification alongside the G-02 events/jobs contract: time, retries, cancellation, ownership and restart; no distributed topology claim.

### Preconditions

- job-5 pauses before committing its counter effect; one worker owns a lease and a replacement worker can run on the same host.

### Steps

1. Expire the first lease, restart ownership processing and let the replacement acquire it.
2. Release the stale worker, then complete the replacement attempt.

### Expected outcome

- History identifies lease loss and the replacement attempt; the job ends completed with one effect.

### Evidence to retain

- Lease identities/times, commit concurrency results and final job/counter records.
