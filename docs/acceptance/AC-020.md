## AC-020-B01 — Check field length and duplicate submission identity

- Family: `AC-020`
- Type: `boundary`
- Profiles: `website`
- Requirements: `CMS-017`, `UI-008`
- Layers: `real-provider-integration`, `api-contract`, `ui`
- Security dependencies: `AC-003`, `AC-004`, `AC-012`, `AC-031`

### Profile scope

- `website`: W contact fields, durable acceptance and duplicate suppression only; antispam, rate limits and recipient authorization are prerequisites from AC-003 and AC-031, not cases here.

### Preconditions

- The fixture declares a supported 2,000-character message limit. Two tabs share the same submission identity and valid field values.

### Steps

1. Submit messages of 1,999, 2,000 and 2,001 characters using separate identities.
2. Submit the same valid request concurrently from both tabs, then reuse that identity with changed content.

### Expected outcome

- The first two lengths pass field validation and the third fails without truncation.
- Identical repeated intent resolves to one request/receipt; changed content under that identity yields an explicit conflict without replacing it.

### Evidence to retain

- Length/input matrix, concurrent responses and request/body digest counts.

## AC-020-N01 — Reject contact field errors and absent routing configuration

- Family: `AC-020`
- Type: `negative`
- Profiles: `website`
- Requirements: `CMS-017`, `UI-008`
- Layers: `real-provider-integration`, `api-contract`, `ui`
- Security dependencies: `AC-003`, `AC-004`, `AC-012`, `AC-031`

### Profile scope

- `website`: W contact fields, durable acceptance and duplicate suppression only; antispam, rate limits and recipient authorization are prerequisites from AC-003 and AC-031, not cases here.

### Preconditions

- The form contract requires a nonempty message and a syntactically valid reply address. A separate fixture has no configured named destination.

### Steps

1. Submit a missing message, then an invalid reply-address syntax.
2. Submit valid fields against the fixture without a destination.

### Expected outcome

- Field errors identify their fields and preserve entered values for correction.
- Missing configuration returns an actionable unavailable outcome; none of the three attempts reports accepted or creates a dispatchable intent.

### Evidence to retain

- Validation/configuration diagnostics, rendered error states and durable request/intent counts.

### Preconditions

- The contact fixture has required message and reply-address fields, a prequalified named destination and passed security prerequisites. The email worker is paused.

## AC-020-R01 — Recover a contact submission with a lost acknowledgement

- Family: `AC-020`
- Type: `recovery`
- Profiles: `website`
- Requirements: `CMS-017`, `UI-008`
- Layers: `real-provider-integration`, `api-contract`, `ui`, `operational-recovery`
- Security dependencies: `AC-003`, `AC-004`, `AC-012`, `AC-031`

### Profile scope

- `website`: W contact fields, durable acceptance and duplicate suppression only; antispam, rate limits and recipient authorization are prerequisites from AC-003 and AC-031, not cases here.

### Preconditions

- contact-5 commits its request and outbox intent, but the HTTP response can be dropped; dispatch is paused.

### Steps

1. Drop the response after commit, restart the application and retry with the original submission identity.
2. Resume dispatch and inspect the durable receipt and downstream delivery state.

### Expected outcome

- Retry recovers the same accepted request with no second intent.
- Email proceeds independently through its observable states under AC-013, and acceptance is retained through restart.

### Evidence to retain

- Fault timing, retry/receipt transcript and before/after request, outbox and delivery identities.
