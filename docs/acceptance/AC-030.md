# AC-030

Planning cases; see [corpus conventions](README.md). These are not execution results.

## AC-030-B01 — Measure the declared load and queue-capacity edges

- Family: `AC-030`
- Type: `boundary`
- Profiles: `website`
- Requirements: `OPS-016`, `OPS-017`, `OPS-018`, `FND-022`
- Layers: `real-provider-integration`, `operational-recovery`
- Security dependencies: `AC-004`, `AC-031`

### Profile scope

- `website`: W small-site workload qualification for authoring, publish/delivery and coordinated restore on the declared single-host container tuple; security resource-abuse assurance remains AC-031 work.

### Preconditions

- The supported fixture capacity is 20 concurrent readers, two authors and a 100-item publication-work queue; admission/backpressure semantics are recorded before measurement.

### Steps

1. Run reader loads of 19, 20 and 21 against the same dataset, recording the declared-capacity boundary.
2. Pause dispatch and submit 99, 100 and 101 distinct due work items in separate runs.

### Expected outcome

- Results distinguish within-capacity measurements from overload; no evidence extends the release claim to unqualified load.
- Queue outcomes match the declared capacity/backpressure contract, with each accepted item durable and every excess item explicitly handled.

### Evidence to retain

- Load/capacity matrix, latency/backlog series, admission outcomes and durable work counts.

## AC-030-N01 — Expose resource pressure and a budget failure

- Family: `AC-030`
- Type: `negative`
- Profiles: `website`
- Requirements: `OPS-016`, `OPS-017`, `OPS-018`
- Layers: `real-provider-integration`, `operational-recovery`
- Security dependencies: `AC-004`, `AC-031`

### Profile scope

- `website`: W small-site workload qualification for authoring, publish/delivery and coordinated restore on the declared single-host container tuple; security resource-abuse assurance remains AC-031 work.

### Preconditions

- The same declared workload and budgets are retained. The test can delay filesystem reads enough to exceed the delivery budget while leaving data intact.

### Steps

1. Inject the recorded delay during the measured interval and continue authoring and publication.
2. Inspect latency, backlog, operation outcomes and the capability report.

### Expected outcome

- The measured budget failure is reported with its fault conditions and cannot be summarized as a passing run.
- Backlog and rejected/failed operations remain visible; no successful response conceals a lost committed save or publication.

### Evidence to retain

- Fault timing, raw latency/resource/backlog samples, operation reconciliation and failing budget verdict.

## AC-030-P01 — Measure a declared small-site workload

- Family: `AC-030`
- Type: `positive`
- Profiles: `website`
- Requirements: `OPS-016`, `OPS-017`, `CMS-022`
- Layers: `real-provider-integration`, `operational-recovery`
- Security dependencies: `AC-004`, `AC-031`

### Profile scope

- `website`: W small-site workload qualification for authoring, publish/delivery and coordinated restore on the declared single-host container tuple; security resource-abuse assurance remains AC-031 work.

### Preconditions

- Use one site/culture, 100 pages with three revisions each, 20 blog posts, two documentation versions and 100 MiB of media. The release-approved budget sheet fixes numeric p95 authoring/delivery latency, publish-to-convergence time, error rate, peak memory and restore-time limits before the run. Hardware, runtime, container and provider versions are pinned.

### Steps

1. Warm up for five minutes, then run 20 concurrent readers and two authors for 15 minutes, each author saving once per minute and publishing every fifth save.
2. Restore the coordinated dataset into the qualified recovery fixture and time integrity verification and readiness.

### Expected outcome

- Report each measured percentile, throughput, error count, resource peak, convergence delay and restore duration against its predeclared limit.
- A pass requires all applicable budgets and content/integrity assertions to hold on this tuple; missing budget inputs prevent qualification.

### Evidence to retain

- Exact workload/budget sheet, hardware/version inventory, raw timed samples, resource series, restore digest report and per-budget verdicts.

## AC-030-R01 — Measure recovery under the declared website workload

- Family: `AC-030`
- Type: `recovery`
- Profiles: `website`
- Requirements: `OPS-016`, `OPS-017`, `CMS-022`, `FND-022`, `FND-024`
- Layers: `real-provider-integration`, `operational-recovery`
- Security dependencies: `AC-004`, `AC-031`

### Profile scope

- `website`: W small-site workload qualification for authoring, publish/delivery and coordinated restore on the declared single-host container tuple; security resource-abuse assurance remains AC-031 work.

### Preconditions

- The positive-case workload and budget sheet are fixed; a coordinated backup and a completed baseline measurement exist.

### Steps

1. During load restart the application after a committed publication and before cache convergence.
2. Reconcile queued work, rebuild cache and repeat the same measured interval.
3. Rehearse restore from the backup and compare time to readiness and data integrity with the declared recovery budget.

### Expected outcome

- Committed work is retained and cache converges to the active publication.
- Measurements expose outage and recovery duration as well as steady-state results; restore meets its declared budget and integrity checks or reports a specific qualification failure.

### Evidence to retain

- Restart and recovery timeline, operation/manifest comparison, repeated workload samples and timed restore integrity results.
